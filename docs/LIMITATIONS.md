# Known limits

## Not yet

- Docs line spacing offers Single, 1.5 and Double; the engine does not take
  1.15 from a command. Columns are not offered: the engine ignores the page
  column command.
- Docs page numbers move or come off from Aa, but a number sitting beside
  words of a person's own in a header or footer stays; and a header or
  footer they leave empty stays on the page, empty.
- Print writes a PDF and hands it to the desktop's PDF app, which prints.
- Sheets has no chart and no borders: the engine opens its own chart wizard,
  which cannot be shown, and its border command needs more work. No fill
  handle drag; typing goes into the chosen cell, with no in-place cell editor
  of our own.
- Slides has no looks (themes), slide backgrounds or transitions between
  slides yet: the engine does not take them from simple commands. Speaker
  notes are not shown while editing or presenting.
- Slides moves and resizes a chosen box only with a mouse drag; the pearls at
  its corners do not drag yet.
- The presenter's view on a second screen has not been tried on a real
  second screen.
- Link in the selection bar uses the engine's link command; it may not take
  an address on every engine version.
- Rename and a document's comments list are not in More yet.
- A file with a password does not open.
- Icons follow the colour scheme in two inks; the desk follows the scheme, the
  page stays the document's own colour.
- The engine runs inside the program; a crash in it takes the window with it.
- Not yet a Flatpak.

## Not planned

- Tracked changes, mail merge, macros and forms.
- Real-time editing by several people.
