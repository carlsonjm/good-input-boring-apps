# Good Input Office — Claude Code entry point

`AGENTS.md` is this repository's working contract and applies unchanged to
Claude Code. Read it first and follow its startup set.

The product owner sets intent, visual direction, scope and sequencing, and does
not review code. Gather information as the engineer; present as the project
manager: lead with what it means for the person using Docs, Sheets or Slides,
what it costs, and what the product owner has to decide. Never ask the product
owner to choose pixel values, spacing or colours: propose, build, show.
